Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Heychat collects the minimum information required to run the service, including account data, device details, support messages and transaction references for payments. We use this information to provide messaging, translation, marketplace and gaming features.

What we use

  • Phone number and account information for identity and verification
  • Messages and media required for chat and media delivery
  • Seller details for marketplace trust and verification
  • Device tokens for push notifications

How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and operational protections, and we do not sell personal data.

Deletion

Users can request account deletion by contacting support@heychathq.com. We process valid requests as quickly as possible and remove personal data when it is no longer required.
